L41: By ICLR 2021 Program Co-Chairs Ivan Titov (U. Edinburgh / U. Amsterdam), Naila Murray (Facebook AI Research), and Alice Oh (KAIST), and Senior Program Chair Katja Hofmann (Microsoft Research)
L42: We are thrilled to announce the winners of the ICLR 2021 Outstanding Paper Awards. While there are 860 excellent papers in our program, and many of them of exceptional quality, we would like to highlight 8 papers that are especially noteworthy.
L43: 
L44: Award winners (in alphabetical order):
L45:   * 【8†Beyond Fully-Connected Layers with Quaternions: Parameterization of Hypercomplex Multiplications with 1/n Parameters†openreview.net】 by Aston Zhang, Yi Tay, Shuai Zhang, Alvin Chan, Anh Tuan Luu, Siu Hui, and Jie Fu
L46:   * 【9†Complex Query Answering with Neural Link Predictors†openreview.net】 by Erik Arakelyan, Daniel Daza, Pasquale Minervini, and Michael Cochez
L47:   * 【10†EigenGame: PCA as a Nash Equilibrium†openreview.net】 by Ian Gemp, Brian McWilliams, Claire Vernade, and Thore Graepel
L48:   * 【11†Learning Mesh-Based Simulation with Graph Networks†openreview.net】 by Tobias Pfaff, Meire Fortunato, Alvaro Sanchez-Gonzalez, and Peter Battaglia
L49:   * 【12†Neural Synthesis of Binaural Speech from Mono Audio†openreview.net】 by Alexander Richard, Dejan Markovic, Israel D. Gebru, Steven Krenn, Gladstone Alexander Butler, Fernando Torre, and Yaser Sheikh
L50:   * 【13†Optimal Rates for Averaged Stochastic Gradient Descent under Neural Tangent Kernel Regime†openreview.net】 by Atsushi Nitanda, and Taiji Suzuki
L51:   * 【14†Rethinking Architecture Selection in Differentiable NAS†openreview.net】 by Ruochen Wang, Minhao Cheng, Xiangning Chen, Xiaocheng Tang, and Cho-Jui Hsieh
L52:   * 【15†Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations†openreview.net】 by Yang Song, Jascha Sohl-Dickstein, Diederik P Kingma, Abhishek Kumar, Stefano Ermon, and Ben Poole
L53: Congratulations to the authors on this fantastic achievement!
L54: 
L55: The paper awards were selected as follows:
L56:   * based on paper reviews, we constructed a shortlist of candidate papers;
L57:   * the shortlisted papers were sent to the Outstanding Paper Committee for further review;
L58:   * the experts were asked to assess not only technical quality but also the potential for impact, including bringing in a new perspective, opening up an exciting direction, and making a strong contribution to addressing an important problem;
L59:   * the highest-ranking papers were selected for the awards.
L60: Thank you to the members of the Outstanding Paper Award Committee for their hard work!
