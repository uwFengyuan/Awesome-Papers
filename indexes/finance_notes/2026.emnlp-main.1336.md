## Abstract / 摘要

Reranking, the second stage of modern retrieval pipelines, is computationally expensive when performed by Large Language Models (LLMs). Existing efficiency-oriented approaches either rely on smaller models or restrict input length, both at the cost of effectiveness. Inspired by recent advances in soft document compression for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), we introduce **RRK**, an efficient and effective listwise reranker that operates directly on multi-token compressed document representations rather than raw text. Trained via simple distillation from a strong teacher, RRK combines rich compressed representations with listwise reranking to deliver a highly efficient yet competitive system: despite its 7B-parameter backbone, it runs 5-20 faster than smaller rerankers (0.6-4B parameters) while matching or outperforming them in effectiveness. The advantage widens further on long-document benchmarks, where input compression yields the largest speedups.
